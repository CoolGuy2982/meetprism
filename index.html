Questions.

What is Prism?

A little lamp that understands the work. There is a projector and a camera inside. It projects a computer onto your desk that you can touch, and it sees whatever is physically on the desk: paper, books, an instrument, your hands.

What can it see?

The surface under the lamp. It reads handwriting, recognizes books and objects, and tracks your hands so you can touch what it draws.

Is it a tutor?

Tutoring is one thing it does, and the one that surprises people most. Underneath, it's a computer that can see your desk. Music, reading, games, maps and the web run on the same lamp.

Does it work on any desk?

It clamps to the edge of the desk you already have, wood or white or anything in between. A flat surface and a little room over it is all it needs.
